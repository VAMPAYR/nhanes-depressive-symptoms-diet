# Fixed secondary analyses, 2 November 2026

This specification was fixed after the original primary results were known and before the secondary analyses were executed. These analyses are exploratory sensitivity analyses. The original primary, all-interval and quadratic fits remain unchanged.

All three secondary outcome models retain exactly the original 2,320 participants in the primary 3 to 10 day domain. The runner records the SHA256 identity of the ordered participant keys without saving the keys. The outcome, weights, original covariates, survey parent and inference policy match the primary analysis. The parent contains every positive WTDR2D record with valid survey identifiers, before domain restriction. Weights equal WTDR2D / 4. Each Gaussian identity model uses its stored survey-model residual degrees of freedom for t intervals and Wald F tests. Models must have full rank, converge and produce finite covariance. Failure stops execution without model substitution.

1. Add only the interaction between PHQ score divided by five and the oriented recall-order sign. Retain the PHQ and order main effects. Report PHQ 10 versus 0 contrasts separately when the beverage-positive recall is the first recall (sign -1) and the second recall (sign +1), their full covariance, and the second-minus-first contrast. Test the interaction with a one-degree-of-freedom Wald F test. Order and interview mode remain inseparable.
2. Add only the interaction between PHQ score divided by five and sex. Retain all original main effects. Report PHQ 10 versus 0 contrasts for males and females, their full covariance, and the female-minus-male contrast. Test the interaction with a one-degree-of-freedom Wald F test. Adjust the two interaction p values together using Holm's procedure. Subgroup intervals remain unadjusted 95 percent intervals.
3. Replace the PHQ total predictor with the appetite-deleted score, PHQ9_TOTAL minus DPQ050, divided by five. The score ranges from 0 to 24. Use the same 2,320 participants and all original covariates, without interactions. Report the coefficient per five score points and its corresponding 10 versus 0 contrast with full covariance and 95 percent intervals. The score is a sensitivity measure without diagnostic thresholds; it is not the PHQ-8.

Describe recall support among adults aged at least 20 years with a strict complete nine-item PHQ score, two reliable valid recalls, positive WTDR2D and valid survey identifiers. For each sex and PHQ band (0 to 4, 5 to 9, at least 10), report the counts and survey-weighted percentages of neither recall positive, first recall only positive, second recall only positive, and both recalls positive. Percentages use each sex-band cell as their denominator. Retain denominator counts, weight denominators and missing-pattern counts. These are unadjusted descriptions, without risk models, confidence intervals or significance tests. Do not infer incident drinking or a population PHQ association from the discordant analysis.

Execute exactly three new outcome fits. Do not fit additional interaction combinations, change the interval domain, select models, impute values, replace the original fits or compute equal-order profiles. Preserve all secondary checkpoints and outputs separately.
